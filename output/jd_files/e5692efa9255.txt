CVM Specialist | Job DescriptionVISUAL MERCHANDISING:
• Partnering closely with the Retail teams to ensure in-store presentation is always at its optimum and elevated. 
• Partner with Global CVM and Market stakeholders to align on collection placement within all stores and implement floorplan moves by working with the Retail teams. 
• Ensure styling guidelines and the merchandising strategy is upheld in all stores, including partnering with Retail teams to identify any visual merchandising (VM) collateral needs. CREATIVE CAMPAIGNS
• Manage the creative campaign ordering process, logistics and installation for all stores, along with any local production needs. Partner with Global CVM on all requests for damages, missing collateral and any changes to the creative orders. 
• Partner with Retail teams and Market stakeholders on creative campaign installation and maintenance training plans. OPERATIONS
• Oversee vendors in-market, including sourcing, onboarding, procurement process (when applicable) and invoice management. NEW STORE OPENINGS AND RENOVATIONS
• Partner with Global, Regional and Market stakeholders on the ordering, logistics and installing of CVM collateral and creatives. 
• Work with Regional and Market stakeholders on VM-related small works projects. RETAIL ACTIVATION & IN-STORE ENVIRONMENTS
• Supporting in-store activations with floor plans, product selection and production. EVENTS & EXHIBITIONS
• Communicate and partner with Global CVM and Market stakeholders on renderings, budget, production, vendor management and implementation. The ideal candidate will:
• Have experience in merchandising, visual display, styling, or related areas, prefeably luxury retail. 
• Be knowledgeable of local vendor sources for display supplies, props, furniture, flowers. 
• Have exceptional organisational and communication skills. 
• Be ambitious, flexible, outgoing and enthusiastic. 
• Be able to work with a variety of people, present ideas and information to groups. 
• Have good knowledge of Microsoft Office (Outlook, MS Excel, Word, Powerpoint) and Adobe Illustrator.
